Privacy

Nexus and the Commander Grimoire Discord bot. Last updated November 8, 2026.

Nexus is a private app for one group of friends who play Magic: The Gathering together. It is run by the group's organizer, not by a company. Nothing in it is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything except running the group's games, game nights and trips.

What is kept

WhatWhy
Your Discord user ID, and the display name Discord shows when you sign inTo know it's you when you sign in, and to show your name
Which Grimoire player and which MagicCon name you claimedSo only you can answer and make changes as yourself
Game results from Playgroup.gg (players, decks, results, notes)Standings, battle history and decks
Game nights, RSVPs, battle stories, MagicCon picks, results, photos, reactions, comments and change requestsThe features you use; a change log records who changed what
A sign-in token on your deviceKeeps you signed in for 90 days. Signing out deletes it

Where it is kept

In the organizer's Google Sheets and Google Drive (photos), which only the organizer can open. Discord commands pass through the organizer's Cloudflare Worker, which keeps nothing. Commander art and card details are loaded from Scryfall. The app's own pages are hosted on GitHub Pages.

Who can see it

Members of the group, through the app and the Discord server. A game night's address is shown only to its host and to people who said they're in, and is never posted to Discord.

Changing or removing your data

Ask the organizer in the group's Discord server. They can unlink your Discord account, release a claimed name, and remove your entries. Signing out of the app removes the sign-in token from that device.

If something goes wrong

If the organizer learns that data was exposed, they will tell the group in Discord, and tell Discord if their developer terms require it.
